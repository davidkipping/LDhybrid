`aa.cls`, `aa.bst`, `linenoaa.sty` and `lineno.sty` here are the official
A&A LaTeX macro package v9.4 (March 2026, EDP Sciences), from
https://www.aanda.org/for-authors ("A&A LaTeX macro package",
macro-latex-aa.zip), installed 2026-10-11.

v9.4 notes: the corresponding author is marked with `\corrauth{email}` in
`\author` (it becomes a footnote); `\email` is used only for metadata
extraction and is ignored in the PDF.
